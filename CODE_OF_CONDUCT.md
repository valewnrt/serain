# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ and applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate, also when you disagree. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Do not publish anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, messages) without their explicit permission. In a project that handles personal messages, this matters twice.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Consequences follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers. Use GitHub's private reporting in the repository's **Security** tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and state that your report concerns the Code of Conduct, or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Do not report conduct issues in public threads. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
